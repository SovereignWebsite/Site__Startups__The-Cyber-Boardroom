The Cyber Boardroom

Cyber readiness for boards of directors
Originthecyberboardroom.com
Sheet checked2026-11-20

Specification

The Cyber Boardroom gives board members an AI cyber security advisor called Athena, short videos and curated articles, so they can understand their organisation's cyber security agenda and fulfil their duties as directors.

The Cyber Boardroom is built for boards of directors. Its stated mission is "to help you understand your organisation's cyber security agenda, as well as fulfil your duties as a board member."1

At its centre is Athena, an AI advisor built on large language models to answer board members' questions about cyber security and the risks their organisation faces.2

Around Athena sit curated articles and a library of 60-second videos on key cyber security topics, which frame cyber security as "a risk management conversation", not an IT one.3 4 5

The site is labelled "community edition" and "beta". A Community Edition can be installed with pip or Docker.1 6

Stated purpose: “Welcome to The Cyber Boardroom, the one-stop shop that boards of directors rely on for their cyber readiness.” (The welcome on thecyberboardroom.com)1

Intended users

User 1Boards of directors and individual board members.1
User 2Directors and executives who need to understand cyber risk without being technical, through CFO and CISO personas among others.9 13
User 3Anyone who wants to run it themselves: the Community Edition installs with pip or Docker.6

Process

  1. Ask AthenaA board member asks a question; Athena, built on large language models, answers about cyber security only.2
  2. Make it yoursBoard members personalise Athena by adding prompts in their profile.1
  3. Choose a modelThe site works with several LLM platforms, including Groq, Mistral, OpenAI and Ollama.14
  4. Learn in minutesCurated articles and 60-second videos cover the topics boards need.3 4

Components

Athena"Your AI cyber security advisor", named after the Greek goddess of wisdom, courage and strategic warfare.2 15
Curated contentArticles including Cyber security in the boardroom, Incident management and The importance of digital trust.3
60-second videosA library of short videos on key cyber security topics.4
Board personasCFO and CISO personas to practise the conversation from each side.13
Chat with LLMsTry several large language models side by side, without a system prompt.14
Community EditionInstall it yourself with pip or Docker; the packages are MIT licensed on PyPI.6 10
