The story of

The Cyber Boardroom

The Cyber Boardroom gives board members an AI cyber security advisor called Athena, short videos and curated articles, so they can understand their organisation's cyber security agenda and fulfil their duties as directors.

The Cyber Boardroom came from a question every director faces: what does cyber security mean for my organisation, and what should I do about it? Its own content answers that it is "a risk management conversation."5

The first videos, introducing the platform and its advisor Athena, are dated February 2024. On 8 April 2024 its founder Dinis Cruz posted that "The Cyber Boardroom LTD has been created, it's official now".7 8

In an interview published by SC Media UK on 22 April 2024, the platform was described as "serverless and driven by GenAI bots and agents".9

July 2024 brought the first MVP write-up and the Community Edition, which anyone can install. The open-source packages behind the site kept being released until January 2025.8 10 11

Today The Cyber Boardroom Limited, registered in the UK, is named on sgit.ai as the commercial vehicle behind sgit.ai and RiskMandate.ai.12

How it works

  1. Ask AthenaA board member asks a question; Athena, built on large language models, answers about cyber security only.2
  2. Make it yoursBoard members personalise Athena by adding prompts in their profile.1
  3. Choose a modelThe site works with several LLM platforms, including Groq, Mistral, OpenAI and Ollama.14
  4. Learn in minutesCurated articles and 60-second videos cover the topics boards need.3 4

What it offers

  • Athena

    "Your AI cyber security advisor", named after the Greek goddess of wisdom, courage and strategic warfare.2 15

  • Curated content

    Articles including Cyber security in the boardroom, Incident management and The importance of digital trust.3

  • 60-second videos

    A library of short videos on key cyber security topics.4

  • Board personas

    CFO and CISO personas to practise the conversation from each side.13

  • Chat with LLMs

    Try several large language models side by side, without a system prompt.14

  • Community Edition

    Install it yourself with pip or Docker; the packages are MIT licensed on PyPI.6 10
