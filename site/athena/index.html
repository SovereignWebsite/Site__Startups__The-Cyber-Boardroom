The 2024 platform

Athena, the AI cyber advisor

Before the board pack, The Cyber Boardroom was a platform for board members to ask about cyber security in their own terms, built around Athena8,1. SC Media UK called it "serverless and driven by GenAI bots and agents"9. It is still online.

What it offered

  • Athena

    "Your AI cyber security advisor", named after the Greek goddess of wisdom, courage and strategic warfare.1

  • Curated content

    Articles including Cyber security in the boardroom, Incident management and The importance of digital trust.2

  • Board personas

    CFO and CISO personas to practise the conversation from each side.3

  • Chat with LLMs

    Several large language models side by side.4

  • Six languages

    Athena answering in six languages.5

  • Community Edition

    Install it yourself with pip or Docker.6,7

What carries over

Athena's job, translating cyber risk for the board, becomes the translation layer of the board pack10.
