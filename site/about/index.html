About

The company underneath

The Cyber Boardroom Limited is a private limited company registered in the UK at Companies House, company number 15623827, incorporated on 7 April 2024 and active6. It is "today the commercial vehicle behind sgit.ai and RiskMandate.ai"1.

Open by default

Most of the work is public. The the-cyber-boardroom organisation on GitHub holds The Cyber Boardroom's own code (the community edition, the content, the user services) and the SG/Send, SGraph and MGraph code7,8,9,3. The investor material, a business plan and pitch decks from early 2025, is in the open too, in cbr-investment10.

The founder

Dinis Cruz founded The Cyber Boardroom, sgit.ai, sgraph.ai, MyFeeds.ai, RiskMandate.ai and VoiceDebrief.ai1. SC Media UK described him as "an experienced CISO for multiple businesses"11.
