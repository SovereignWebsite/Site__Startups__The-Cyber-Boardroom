The product

Board packs

A board pack is what a board reads before it decides. The Cyber Boardroom's is about cyber and AI risk: what the organisation accepts, what its agents may do, and the evidence, delivered so that each director can open it and nobody else can1,2.

Four parts, each already built somewhere

  1. The vault: the pack's container

    Each pack lives in an SG/Send vault: zero-knowledge encrypted file sharing3. The design already lists "board pack distribution", where "each board member gets a personalised view encrypted to their key"2, and its vault previews were drawn with an "Acme Q3 Board Pack" as the example4.

  2. The risk register: what the board accepts

    A "Risk Register Executive View" brief sets two hard rules: "simulated acceptance indelibly distinct from real (cryptographic or structural separator, not a boolean flag)", and "roles not named individuals"5. A director should never mistake a rehearsal for a decision.

  3. Agent Behaviour Policies: what the AI agents may do

    RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy for each agent: what it can actually do, what you authorised it to do, and what stands in the way of the difference6. The pack gives the board the gaps, in the board's words.

  4. The translation: the board's language

    The Cyber Boardroom's own work since 2024: Athena, an AI cyber security advisor, CFO and CISO personas, and articles such as "Cyber security in the boardroom"7,8,9. Its 2026 thesis: board members "need translation, not education"10.

A first outline

A proposal for the first pack, to be shaped with the first boards. Not a customer's pack.

Board pack: cyber and AI risk

  1. Cover note. What the board is asked to decide, in one page.
  2. Paper A: the risk register. The risks held, by role, with each acceptance marked real or simulated.
  3. Paper B: risks for decision. The ones to accept, reduce or refuse this time.
  4. Paper C: AI agents. Each agent's behaviour policy: what it can do against what was authorised, and the gaps.
  5. Evidence. The vault: every claim links to its record, readable by the directors who hold the key.

For whom

The 2024 platform was made for board members and for the CISOs who brief them11,10. The pack serves both sides of that conversation. If you sit on a board or brief one, talk to us.
